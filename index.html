🔍 Preview Allocation

Read-only dry run using the exact same allocation logic as "Process All Bids". Nothing is saved, bidding is not marked as processed, and no staff notification is sent. Corporate Staff are excluded (they bid separately).

📋 Bid Allocation Justification Report

One row per awarded slot, explaining exactly why it was awarded — which choice number it was, and (when an earlier preference was lost) who won it instead by seniority. Covers Ops and Maintenance staff, who bid on ranked slot choices; Golden Command / Corporate Staff use a separate free-date process and aren't included.
